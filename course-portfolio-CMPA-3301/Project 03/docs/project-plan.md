# Project Plan

It’s important to calculate the risks involved in a project. For project 03, the biggest risk that threatened whether I could complete it or not was running out of time. I knew this week was going to be demanding in work and my social life, so I gave the severity of this risk a 5. The grade I receive for this assignment will affect my overall class performance and could even lower my GPA from a 4.0 to a 3.9. The probability of this risk occurring was scored at a 3. With a score of 15, I needed to TAME this risk by mitigating it.

I was able to request an alteration in my work schedule to reduce my hours. This gave me more time to work on my final.
